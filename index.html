Common Tomato Leaf Diseases

Early Blight

Caused by the fungus Alternaria solani. It causes dark, concentric spots on the leaves.

Late Blight

Caused by Phytophthora infestans. It leads to large, irregular, water-soaked lesions.

Bacterial Spot

Caused by Xanthomonas campestris. It causes dark, water-soaked lesions on leaves.

Septoria Leaf Spot

Caused by Septoria lycopersici. It causes small, round, dark spots on the leaves.
